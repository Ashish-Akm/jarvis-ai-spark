## 🌟 Overview

**Jarvis AI Spark** (also known as *Jarvis Spark* / *Jarvis AI*) controls your entire workstation through natural voice intent. Unlike simple browser-bound chatbots, Jarvis hooks directly into workstation accessibility pipelines, local neural models, shell command execution, and long-term encrypted memory.
